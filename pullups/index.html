Nov 22
Tabata and Superset

Warmup:

  • 5 arch hangs
  • 5 /side World’s Greatest Stretch
  • 5 arm circles each way, then prone Y-T-W
  • 5 squat sky reaches

Then:

  • 4min Tabata alternating slow bodyweight squats and mountain climbers

Then 3 rounds superset:

  • 5 pull-ups
  • tire-swing rows: 10 , then 6 , then 3 +3
  • 10 push-ups with a 3s lowering
  • 8 /leg single-leg glute bridges
  • 30s /side side plank

Nov 20
Tabata, Superset, and Swim

Warmup:

  • 10 arch hangs
  • 5 /side World’s Greatest Stretch
  • 5 squat sky reaches
  • Arm circles and prone Y-T-W
  • 10 kneeling lean backs

Then:

  • 4min Tabata alternating push-ups and reverse lunges

Then 3 rounds superset:

  • 6 /leg rear foot elevated split squats
  • 5 pull-ups
  • 25s plank

Then:

  • 10 two-legged slow calf raises holding a tire swing
  • Swim, about 15 laps

Pullups, Paleo

Started the “Master the Pullup” plan on Lift. I skipped the first week, so today’s workout was:

  • 6 Pull-Up
  • 5 Chin-Up
  • 4 Neutral Pull-Up
  • 6 Chin-Up

Diana and I also started a Paleo diet for the next 4 weeks as part of Lift’s Quatified Diet experiment. Each person gets randomly assigned to one of 10 diets, and they track how you do (how you feel, your weight, etc). Paleo was one of the more restrictive diets, and also one of the most different from my normal diet. So this should be fun.
